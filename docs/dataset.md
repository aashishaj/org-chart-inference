## 7. Known simplifications

- One manager per person: no matrix or dotted-line reporting.
- One snapshot in time: no end dates, so ghosts are only detectable through clues (older start date, duplicated role).
- Every company has a single CEO at the top; holding companies and co-CEOs aren't modelled.
- Names and employer names are fictional; cities, sales regions, shopping centres and mine sites are real places.
- Titles are English only.
